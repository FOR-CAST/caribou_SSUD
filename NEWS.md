# caribou_SSUD (development version)

- The northern mountain ecotype predicts from a table of the iSSA model's fixed-effect coefficients (`iSSAcoefficients`: columns `term` and `estimate`, e.g. hsaCEtool's `<model>_fixed.csv`), read from `coefficientsFile` in `outputFolderID` unless supplied, or taken from a supplied `iSSAmodels`. `iSSAmodels` is downloaded only for the boreal ecotype.
